## 0. 一句總結 ⭐⭐⭐

1. **Emotive vs Neutral language**：一句說話可以同時 **report facts** 又 **express feelings**；用字可以影響聽者嘅 **attitudes**。
2. **Disagreement in belief vs Disagreement in attitude**：爭論可以係關於**事實的信念**，或者係對**已同意的事實的態度**。
3. **三類爭論 (Three categories of disputes)**：
   - **Obviously genuine dispute**（明顯真正嘅爭論）
   - **Merely verbal dispute**（純粹字面之爭）
   - **Apparently verbal but really genuine dispute**（表面係字面之爭，實際係真正爭論）

## 1. §2 Emotive Language, Neutral Language, and Disputes ⭐⭐

### 1.1 語言嘅多重功能
- > "Because a given sentence, or passage, can serve **several functions**—that is, for example, it can **express feelings while reporting facts**—the **clever** use of language can be **deceptive or manipulative**, and the **careless** use of language can lead to **needless misunderstanding and dispute**."
- 用嚟傳達信念嘅字可以係 **neutral and exact**，但亦可以（無意或有意）**影響聽者嘅態度 (attitudes)**。

### 1.2 例子 ⭐
| 例子 | 重點 |
|---|---|
| **"A rose by any other name would smell as sweet"**（**Shakespeare**） | 但如果遞花俾你時話佢通常叫 **"skunkweed"**（臭鼬草），你嘅反應會受影響 |
| **Euphemisms（委婉語）** = "**gentle words for harsh realities**" | 因某啲字引起負面態度而創造：**Janitors → "maintenance workers" → "custodians"**；**"Waiters" → "waitpersons" → "servers"** |
| 醫學詞彙 vs 粗口 | 有關生殖、排泄嘅**醫學詞彙係 neutral、唔冒犯**；但嗰啲 **four-letter words**（粗俗同義詞）令好多人震驚，因為佢哋引起嘅態度 |
| **"Seven dirty words"** | 美國廣播媒體禁用——因為佢哋有 **unacceptable emotive meanings**，同佢哋嘅 **literal meanings** 截然不同 |

### 1.3 幾時用 / 避免 emotive language ⭐⭐
- **Emotionally colored language** 喺某啲脈絡**適當**——例如 **poetry（詩）**；但喺其他脈絡**非常不適當**——例如 **survey research（問卷調查）**（回應好大程度取決於問題用字）。
- ⭐ > "Whether we should avoid emotive language, or rely on it, **depends on the purpose language is intended to serve in the context**."
- 如果目標係 **unbiased report of facts**，用帶強烈情感意義嘅字就會破壞呢個目標。
- 有時**幾乎冇可能避免** emotive content：墮胎爭議雙方自稱 **"pro-life"** 或 **"pro-choice"**。
- ⭐ > "**In logic we generally strive for language that is, so far as possible, free of the distortion that emotive meanings introduce.**"

### 1.4 廣告 & 政治 ⭐
- **Playing on the emotions** 係 **advertising industry** 嘅核心技巧：目的係 persuade and sell，操控態度變成**精密嘅專業藝術**。
- **Political campaigns**：rhetorical tricks 好常見，用字選擇好關鍵。
- ⭐ **最佳防禦**：> "The best defense against trickery, for voters as for consumers, is an **awareness of the real uses to which the language before us is being put**."
- 要提防嗰啲用字令 "**the worse appear the better cause**" 嘅人。
- **Benjamin Disraeli**："**With words we govern men.**"

### 1.5 Disagreement in Belief vs Disagreement in Attitude ⭐⭐⭐
- 爭論雙方嘅分歧可以係：
  1. **Disagreements in beliefs about the facts**（對事實嘅信念有分歧）
  2. **Disagreements in attitude about facts that are actually agreed upon**（事實上同意，但態度有分歧）
- 呢種不確定同混亂可能因為爭論用字有**好唔同嘅 emotive meanings**。

**死刑例子 (Death penalty for murder)** ⭐⭐
- X 同 Y 可以喺**事實**上同意或不同意：**capital punishment 係咪 effective deterrent（有效阻嚇）**？→ **belief**
- 亦可以喺**態度**上同意或不同意：**國家處決犯人係咪 right（啱）**（無論阻嚇效果如何）？→ **attitude**
- 四種組合：

| | Agree in **attitude** | Disagree in **attitude** |
|---|---|---|
| **Agree in belief** | （冇爭論） | ✔ 可能 |
| **Disagree in belief** | ✔ 可能 | ✔ 可能（兩樣都唔同意） |

**點樣解決？** ⭐
- 要**確定雙方真正爭拗嘅係乜 (what really is at issue)**。
- 如果爭論真係關於**死刑是否有阻嚇作用** → 首先要**客觀地確定事實**（雖然未必容易）。
- 如果爭論源於對**國家處決是否正確**嘅**信念衝突** → 就算**事實達成共識都好可能唔足夠**解決爭論。
- 有時 attitude 上嘅分歧**根源於** belief 上嘅分歧；有時**唔係**。

**Grantland Rice vs Vince Lombardi** ⭐
- 記者 **Grantland Rice**："For when the One Great Scorer comes / To write against your name, / He marks—**not that you won or lost— / But how you played the game.**"
- 教練 **Vince Lombardi**："**Winning isn't everything. It's the only thing.**"
- 書問：呢個 **disagreement in attitude** 係咪根源於 **disagreement in belief**？（→ 主要係**態度**分歧：贏嘅重要性）

- ⭐ 總結：> "We do not reach agreement simply by recognizing the nature of the dispute. But **until we recognize the real nature of a dispute, and the differing functions of the language** used by the conflicting parties, it is **unlikely** that the resolution of differences can be achieved."
  - ⚠️ T/F 陷阱：「認清爭論嘅性質就足以達成共識」→ **F**（係**必要**但**唔足夠**）

## 2. §3 Disputes and Ambiguity ⭐⭐⭐

### 2.1 Genuine vs Merely Verbal
- 好多爭論（無論關於 beliefs 定 attitudes）係 **genuine（真正嘅）**。
- 但有啲爭論係 **merely verbal**——**只係因為語言誤解 (linguistic misunderstanding)** 而產生。
- 爭論雙方所用嘅詞可能有多過一個意思——即係 **ambiguous**——而雙方**冇察覺**。
- ⭐ > "To uncover and to resolve verbal disagreements, **ambiguities must be identified**, and the **alternative meanings of the critical terms** in the dispute must be **distinguished and clarified**."（= 語理分析：釐清關鍵用語！）

### 2.2 三類爭論 Three Categories of Disputes ⭐⭐⭐

| 類別 | 定義 | 例子 | 點解決 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Obviously genuine dispute** | 明顯真正嘅分歧 | **A 支持 Yankees，B 支持 Red Sox** → genuine，主要係 **attitude** 分歧；**C 相信 Miami 喺 Honolulu 以南，D 否認** → genuine，關於**地理事實** | 事實爭論：**a good map can settle the matter**；態度分歧未必解決到 |
| **2. Merely verbal dispute** | **表面衝突唔係真嘅**，只要就**某個字詞點理解**達成共識就解決 | **Tree falling in the wilderness**：F 話冇人聽到就**冇 sound**；G 堅持有 sound。若 **"sound" = 人類聽覺感受 (human auditory sensation)** → 冇；若 **"sound" = 空氣振動 (vibrations in the air)** → 有 | **Getting clear about what is meant by "sound"** 就解決 → **no more than verbal** |
| **3. Apparently verbal but really genuine** | 涉及用詞誤解，但**澄清之後仍然有超越字義嘅分歧** | **Film with explicit sexual activity 算唔算 "pornography"？** J：露骨 → pornographic and offensive；K：美感同細膩 → **art, not pornography**。澄清 "pornography" 意思之後，雙方**仍然對套戲嘅判斷有分歧** | 定義可以解決「算唔算 pornographic」，但會暴露**更深層嘅分歧** |

**Pornography 例子細節** ⭐⭐
- "Pornographic" 帶明顯嘅 **pejorative associations（貶義聯想）**。
- J（反對套戲）同 K（認同套戲）**用法唔同**；但對兩人嚟講，個字嘅 **emotional meaning 都好負面**。
- 佢哋分歧在於 **the criteria for the application of that negative word**（應用呢個負面詞嘅**準則**）。
- → 呢個係「**apparently verbal but really genuine**」。

### 2.3 處理爭論嘅程序 (Summary) ⭐⭐⭐（背！）
```
面對爭論
  ↓
Q1: 有冇 ambiguity 可以透過澄清 alternative meanings 而消除？
  ├─ 冇 → genuine dispute（obviously genuine）
  └─ 有 → Q2: 澄清咗語言問題之後，爭論解決咗未？
             ├─ 解決咗 → merely verbal dispute
             └─ 未解決 → genuine dispute（apparently verbal but really genuine）
```
> "When confronting a dispute that arises in discourse, we must **first ask whether there is some ambiguity** that can be eliminated by clarifying the alternative meanings in play. If there is, then we must ask **whether clearing up that linguistic issue will resolve the matter**. If it does, the dispute was indeed **merely verbal**. If it does not, the dispute was **genuine**, although it may have appeared to be merely verbal."

- 搵三個現時政治/社會爭議：一個 genuine、一個 merely verbal、一個 apparently verbal but really genuine，並解釋。
